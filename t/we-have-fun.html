You can feel a clenched room in about four seconds. Everybody's polite, everybody's prepared, nobody's actually in there.

Heavy energy repels people. Treating business development like a factory shift doesn't only feel worse, it works worse, because the energy somebody walks in with is the thing people are actually deciding about. Not the deck. The energy.

People are better at this work when they're enjoying it. Faster, sharper, braver, funnier. That's not a personality note. That's a performance note.

It looks like laughter in a session. Lightness carried into a hard conversation without dodging the hard part. Noticing that the grinding itself has become the problem, and stopping. And changing your energy on purpose instead of waiting on results to come change it for you, which is a long wait and an unreliable one. The work is serious. Nobody has to be.
